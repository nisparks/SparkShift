Credit **Jeff Hertzberg / cmlarsen**, derived from **The DoomMeister** and
**Droftarts**. The nominal GT2 polygon and derived toothed belt liners retain
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
not GPL-only labeling.

Pinned upstream source:
https://github.com/cmlarsen/scad-parametric-timing-belt-generator/blob/5af5748dc378b39274ec00d361eddae2a5b84b66/timing_belt_generator.scad

The nominal profile was adapted into pocket-supported, side-loading liners for
four 9mm belt ends, with seven full working contacts per end. This is a nominal
profile, not supplier tooth metrology. These derived liners retain their
separate license wherever they are included in the preview CAD or meshes.
In the current part list, these are P13 (left toothed belt liner) and P14 (right
toothed belt liner); the [BOM](BOM.md) identifies their separate license scope.
